rheome.flow.demod

function rheome.flow — Rheome

  FLOW.DEMOD  Move the carrier to zero by re-indexing the frequency axis.
 
    [Cd, fd] = rheome.flow.demod(C, f, w0)
 
  Demodulation multiplies by exp(-i*2*pi*w0*t) in time, which in frequency is C(omega) ->
  C(omega + w0): the coefficients are untouched and only the axis moves.
 
  ⚠ WHAT THIS DOES **NOT** DO. exp(-i*2*pi*w0*t) has UNIT MODULUS, so it cannot change any
  magnitude: |Psi*c| is identical with and without it (measured 1.8e-14 on a reference subject), and
  ridge tracking is bit-identical -- 702 ridges either way. The long ridges come from forming the
  ANALYTIC SIGNAL at rheome.flow.joint (retaining positive frequencies only), not from this shift. On the
  same data the real oscillating field gives 17 ridges and the analytic signal 702, with or
  without demodulation.
 
  WHAT IT IS FOR. The PHASE. angle(c) after the shift is measured relative to the carrier, so it
  is interpretable: equal phases mean co-rotating, a difference of pi means counter-rotating, and
  the instantaneous sign of the vorticity is Re(c*exp(i*w0*t)). Without the shift the phase
  carries a w0*t ramp that swamps all of it. Chirality lives here -- and is currently discarded,
  because detection takes abs() and never looks at the angle.
 
  w0 should come from rheome.spectral.carrier applied to the PERIODIC component: estimated on total
  power the carrier depends on where the band edges were put (see spectral/carrier.m).
 
  INPUTS:  C [K x nOmega], f [1 x nOmega] (Hz), w0 carrier (Hz)
  OUTPUT:  Cd == C (unchanged), fd = f - w0
 
  See also: rheome.flow.joint, rheome.flow.synth, rheome.spectral.carrier
