rheome.ingest.cone

function rheome.ingest — Rheome

  INGEST.CONE  Which samples are inside the cone of influence, per band, analytically.
 
    inside = rheome.ingest.cone(nT, fs, frame, fLowest)     -> [nB x nT] logical
 
  The cone frequency at a sample is coi(t) = min(fmax, supportTimesFc / dist(t)), dist
  being the seconds to the nearer RECORD edge and supportTimesFc the wavelet's measured
  support times centre frequency (6.46 for Morse at 10 voices; frame.support.timeTimesFc).
  A band is inside the cone at t iff its lowest scale is: fLowest(b) < coi(t) -- i.e. the
  record edge lies within that scale's support of t.
 
  ⚠ THIS IS WIDER THAN MATLAB'S coi (whose constant is 1.65, frame.coiConst) BY DESIGN.
  Measured on the reference data: a page clipped at the record edge and the whole record extend the
  signal differently there, and their coefficients differ up to ~one support in from
  the edge -- well beyond MATLAB's e-folding cone. The label must cover what it names:
  every tile where the boundary extension shows is inside this cone, and outside it the
  paged and whole-record stores agree to 1e-6 (tests/tIngestPaged.m, notes).
 
  Because it is a function of the record's edges only, the whole-record and the paged
  reduce count the same nCoi -- a page's own edges are covered by its halo.
 
  INPUTS:
    nT, fs    record length and rate
    frame     from rheome.ingest.bank (.support.timeTimesFc, .fmax)
    fLowest   [nB x 1] lowest scale centre per band
  OUTPUT:
    inside    [nB x nT] logical
 
  See also: rheome.ingest.bank, rheome.ingest.reduce, rheome.ingest.reducepaged
